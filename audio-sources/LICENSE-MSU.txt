Processing: selected original MP3 bytes are unchanged. Files are renamed with
their pinyin reading, source speaker and source hash. No trimming, resampling,
gain, normalization or pitch changes. Selection and verification metadata are
Mandarin Grove's additions; original publisher labels remain separately recorded.

Only the finite selected subset in mainland.json is distributed here. The full
private preservation archive, research models and other reference corpora are
not included. The university, publisher and speakers do not endorse this app.
Recordings demonstrate isolated adult Putonghua syllables. They do not certify
connected speech, all regional accents or children's pronunciation assessment.
