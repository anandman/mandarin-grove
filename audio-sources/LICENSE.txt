Mandarin Grove selects 557 publisher-labeled readings. 548 MP3 files are
unchanged. Nine neutral-tone IEEE Float32 WAV files are converted to signed
PCM16 WAV for browser compatibility, preserving every sample frame, the
original 48kHz mono rate and volume. They are not trimmed or resampled.
The manifest records source and distributed hashes and conversion details.

The publisher does not endorse this application. Its personal speaker
identities are not specified in the archive. These are publisher references;
no fluent-listener certification or automated pronunciation guarantee is made.

See cns11643.json for per-file provenance and the two verified replacements
for source archive u/ü duplicates. See cns11643-phonetics.txt for the original,
unchanged publisher mapping and README.md for reproducible import commands.
